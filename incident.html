1. Purpose

The purpose of this Incident Response Plan (IRP) is to provide a standardized response framework for cybersecurity incidents at Vadom Data Consulting SAS. This plan ensures that incidents are detected, analyzed, contained, and eradicated promptly to minimize impact on the business and our clients.

2. Scope

This plan applies to all systems, networks, and data operated by Vadom Data, specifically the Supplier Management Platform hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). It covers incidents ranging from service interruptions to confirmed data breaches.

3. Roles and Responsibilities

  • Incident Commander (IC): The CTO or designee. Responsible for overall coordination and high-level decision-making.
  • SOC Team (Advancit): Responsible for initial detection, triage, and infrastructure containment (Level 1 & 2).
  • Engineering Team: Responsible for application-level analysis, code patches, and data restoration (Level 3).
  • Legal/Compliance: Responsible for regulatory reporting and client notifications.

4. Incident Response Phases (NIST Framework)

Phase 1: Preparation

  • Tools: Maintenance of monitoring systems (AWS CloudWatch, GuardDuty), logging aggregators, and backup systems.
  • Training: Regular tabletop exercises and phishing simulations for staff.

Phase 2: Detection and Analysis

  • Sources: Alerts are generated from SIEM tools, AWS CloudWatch alarms, or user reports.
  • Triage: The SOC Team validates the alert to rule out false positives.
  • Severity Classification:
    • SEV-1 (Critical): Data Breach, active external attack, total system outage.
    • SEV-2 (High): System performance degradation, malware on single endpoint.
    • SEV-3 (Low): Non-critical alerts, failed login attempts.

Phase 3: Containment, Eradication, and Recovery

  • Containment:
    • Short-term: Blocking IP addresses via WAF, isolating EC2 instances, revoking compromised AWS IAM keys.
    • Long-term: Patching vulnerabilities, updating firewall rules.
  • Eradication: Removing malware, deleting malicious accounts, and mitigating the root vulnerability.
  • Recovery: Restoring systems from clean backups (AWS Backup) and verifying system integrity before restoring full service.

Phase 4: Post-Incident Activity

  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA): A mandatory report analyzing why the incident happened and how to prevent recurrence.
  • Lessons Learned: Meeting held within 5 days of incident closure to update policies or tools.

5. Client Notification Policy

In the event of a confirmed security incident affecting client data (Confidentiality/Integrity) or service availability:

  • Timeline: Vadom Data will notify the client’s Point of Contact within 24 hours of breach confirmation.
  • Content: The notification will include the nature of the incident, data affected, and immediate remediation steps taken.

6. Escalation Matrix

  • Level 1: Automated Alert -> SOC Analyst (Advancit).
  • Level 2: SOC Analyst -> Lead Engineer (Vadom).
  • Level 3: Lead Engineer -> CTO / Incident Commander (Vadom).
