1. Purpose

The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to demonstrate the commitment of Vadom Data Consulting SAS ("Vadom Data") to protecting its information assets and those of its clients. This document establishes the high-level framework for the Information Security Management System (ISMS), aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 standards, to ensure the Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) of data.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, third-party vendors (including our Managed Service Provider, Advancit), and systems supporting Vadom Data’s operations, specifically the Supplier Management Platform hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

3. Governance and Roles

  • Chief Technology Officer (CTO): Accountable for the design and implementation of the security strategy.
  • Security Committee: Meets quarterly to review risks and security incidents.
  • External SOC (Advancit): Serves as our specialized operational arm, responsible for 24/7 infrastructure monitoring, threat detection, and Level 2 incident response within the AWS environment.
  • Employees: Responsible for adhering to policies and reporting suspicious activities.

4. Information Risk Management

Vadom Data adopts a risk-based approach aligned with ISO 31000 and NIST SP 800-30.

  • Risk Assessment: Performed annually or upon significant changes. Risks are evaluated based on likelihood and impact.
  • Risk Treatment: Identified risks must be mitigated, transferred, avoided, or accepted (with formal sign-off) based on the organization's risk appetite.

5. Asset Management

  • Inventory: All information assets (hardware, software, data) must be identified and maintained in a centralized register.
  • Classification: Data must be classified (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) and handled according to the Data Classification Matrix (VAD-SEC-POL-002).
  • Acceptable Use: All personnel must sign and adhere to the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) regarding corporate devices and networks.

6. Access Control

  • Principle of Least Privilege: Users are granted the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.
  • User Management: Access rights are reviewed quarterly. Access is immediately revoked upon termination of employment.
  • Authentication (Internal Staff): Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all access to production environments, email, and administrative consoles.
  • Authentication (External Users): Access is governed by secure, time-bound cryptographic tokens (Passwordless Architecture).

7. Cryptography

  • Data at Rest: All sensitive data stored in databases (RDS) and file systems (S3/EBS) must be encrypted using AES-256 standard.
  • Data in Transit: All network communication transmitting sensitive data must be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.

8. Physical and Environmental Security

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Vadom Data leverages AWS data centers (regions US-East/US-West/SA-East), which are ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified.
  • Corporate Offices: Physical access is restricted to authorized personnel via badge access and monitored by CCTV. Clean Desk Policy is enforced.

9. Operations Security

  • Malware Protection: Antivirus/Anti-malware agents installed on all endpoints. AWS Shield protects cloud resources against DDoS attacks.
  • Backups: Automated, encrypted backups of critical data performed daily and tested quarterly.
  • Vulnerability Management: Regular automated scans to identify and remediate platform vulnerabilities.
  • Logging: Audit logs (AWS CloudTrail, App Logs) collected and retained for 12 months.

10. Communications Security

The production environment resides within a Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), segmented into Public and Private subnets to isolate database layers from the internet. Security Groups and Network ACLs strictly filter traffic.

11. Supplier Relationships

  • Vendor Due Diligence: Critical providers (including Advancit) undergo security assessments.
  • Agreements: Security requirements, SLAs, and Right-to-Audit clauses are included in contracts.

12. Incident Management

Employees must report incidents immediately. Incidents are handled per the Incident Response Plan (triage, containment, eradication, recovery) with post-incident reviews.

13. Business Continuity

Vadom Data maintains a BC/DR Plan to ensure critical services restoration within defined RTO/RPO targets, leveraging AWS multi-zone availability.

14. Compliance

  • Legal: Compliance with applicable laws, including Habeas Data (Law 1581 of 2012) and GDPR.
  • Audit: Internal and external audits conducted periodically.
